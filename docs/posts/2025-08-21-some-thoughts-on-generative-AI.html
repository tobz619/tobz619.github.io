A Few Thoughts On Generative AI
Posted on August 22, 2025 by tobi
Keywords: Generative, AI, Creativity
Description: A post about how I feel about generative AI.

What does it mean to be creative?

In God’s case, it took 6 days creating the earth and everything within it. Did it so well, He took one last one to rest. Infinitely powerful, infinitely present, still wanted to take His time and perhaps take some care in the work.

And every day, we can see how all that turned out.

I was talking with a 17 year old the other day about Generative AI. For him, it was exciting to know that people who had ideas and “creativity” but maybe didn’t have the “skill” could take part in creative work:

When I was younger, I was really into this software called Endorphin, and remembered one of my early videos. Endorphin was never going to be future of motion synthesis in media, but it sure made me a lot of memories. It also inspired me to create something, that, even though it’s not perfect – hell it’s not even great compared to the real greatest videos – but it was what I could do. It was all I was capable of.

My masterpiece came when I was a mere 12 years old. Endorphin New Breed Wrestling was my summer holiday project of passion to create and recreate some of the moves that defined my love for all things professional wrestling.

Being able to take part in creative work is such a blessing. I believe life is full of people, who, given the space and time to develop their talents, would go on to develop and produce the most wonderful things. However, just as time and space is not equally distributed amongst us, neither is ability: for one reason or another, we are not equal in opportunity…yet.
