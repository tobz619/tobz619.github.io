### What does it mean to be creative? 

In God's case, it took 6 days creating the earth and everything within it. Did it so well, He took one last one to rest. Infinitely powerful, infinitely present, still wanted to take His time and perhaps take some care in the work.

And every day, we see how all that turned out.

**************************************************

I was talking with a 17 year old the other day about Generative AI. For him, it was exciting to know that people who had ideas and "creativity" but maybe didn't have the "skill" could take part in creative work:

When I was younger, I was really into this software called [Endorphin](https://archive.org/details/natural-motion-endorphin), and remembered one of my early [videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYRZMySoyCA). 
Endorphin was never going to be future of motion synthesis in media, but it sure made me a lot of memories 
and taught me a lot about 3D (Don't be surprised if my game engine has a lot of similar functionality lol). 
It also inspired me to create something, that, even though it's not perfect -- hell it's not even great compared to the real [greatest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yo2Zu73oZe0&pp=ygUYZW5kb3JwaGluIGl0J3MgZGFtbiByZWFs) videos -- 
but it was what I could do. It was all I was capable of at the time. 

My [masterpiece](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eC2oXlsSNMo) came when I was a mere 12 years old, 
16 years ago at the time of writing. 
Endorphin New Breed Wrestling was my summer holiday project of passion to create and recreate some of the moves
that defined my love for all things professional wrestling.

**************************************************

Being able to take part in creative work is such a blessing. I believe life is full of people, who, given the 
space and time to develop their talents, would go on to develop and produce the most wonderful things.
However, just as time and space are not equally distributed amongst us, neither is ability: for one reason or
another, some people are just better at some things than others; and some people have more time to get better at some things than others.

I am in agreement with Rob Miles: [*technology is good*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zATXsGm_xJo) because technology
allows us to do more, better and faster. For those who cannot paint, photography presents the ability to show the world from the beholder's perspective.
For every writer, there can also be movie producer to translate the written word into immersive and moving visuals.

New technology enables us to do more
